Sb4ssport-Alpha: a port for everything I build

The problem. I needed a public shelf: a way to share everything I've built, and to reuse it. I love outer space and the idea of a port, so the name came easily.

What I built. A shelf that also centralizes my own tools. Every repository with an AI agent starts a session the same way: get your bearings, check the working notes and the work log, then ask what we're working on. The agent knows to use the shared tools, like the folder-structure tool, instead of searching blindly. The same parts build this page and its themes.

Where it stands. In progress, opening soon.

What I learned. I want my little ecosystem to be self-similar. My personal organizer takes its shapes and tools from Sb4ssport, and it in turn inspired the shape I use for a family's or a live organization's repository. Everything has to work together, and that takes a lot of polish, plumbing and consideration.
